Our History

A Decade of Purpose

When FCUB opened in Chuadanga, it ended a long wait — no private university had ever served the twenty districts of Khulna Division. Every milestone since has been measured the same way: by the leaders who walk out of our gates.

From a founding batch in 2014 to a community of two thousand learners across five faculties, the story is still being written — one graduating class at a time.

  1. 2013

    The Founding Vision

    A group of educators, entrepreneurs and community leaders in Chuadanga ask a simple question — why should the children of Khulna Division travel hundreds of kilometres for a private university education? The answer becomes a promise.

  2. 2014

    The First Private University in Khulna Division

    First Capital University of Bangladesh opens its doors at Ghoramara, Alamdanga Road, Chuadanga-7200, welcoming its founding batch under the motto “Where Our Learners Become Leaders.”

  3. 2016

    Five Faculties, Eight Departments

    The university expands from its first programs into five faculties — Science & Engineering Technology, Business Administration, Law, Arts & Social Science, and Agriculture.

  4. 2018

    A Campus of Our Own

    A purpose-built academic campus takes shape — excellent computer labs, a rich central library, and spacious, well-ventilated classrooms set among green lawns.

  5. 2021

    Two Thousand Learners Strong

    Enrolment crosses 2,000 students. Convocation days, research showcases and inter-university championships put FCUB firmly on the regional map.

  6. Today

    Where Learners Become Leaders

    A politics-free campus community guided by the Board of Trustees, the Academic Council and a full-time faculty devoted to one goal: education for excellence.

Vision & Mission

The Ideas That Move Us

01

Our Vision

To be recognised as the leading centre of learning in southern Bangladesh — a university whose graduates are known across the nation for their knowledge, character and capacity to lead.

First in the division. First in our hearts.

02

Our Mission

To provide excellent, affordable and politics-free higher education — cultivating skilled, ethical graduates through dedicated full-time faculty, modern laboratories, and a curriculum tuned to the demands of the 21st-century workplace.

Education for excellence, every single day.

The Values We Keep

Four commitments, printed on no wall but practised in every classroom, corridor and committee room.

01

Integrity

Honesty in scholarship, fairness in examination and transparency in everything we administer.

02

Excellence

Rigorous curricula and full-time faculty who hold every classroom to an international standard.

03

Innovation

Labs, studios and societies where students test ideas, build prototypes and question conventions.

04

Service

Learning that returns to its community — from relief drives to free coaching for local schools.

Message from the Vice-Chancellor

“We measure our success not in buildings, but in graduates — young people who leave this campus ready to lead with knowledge, integrity and compassion.”
