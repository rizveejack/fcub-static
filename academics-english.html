Department of English

Literature, linguistics and communication — the liberal education that opens global career doors.

Overview

The Craft of Expression

Every profession rewards the person who writes clearly, reads carefully and speaks with confidence. The Department of English builds those capacities to an international standard while giving students a deep, humane education in literature and language.

The B.A. (Hons.) spans Chaucer to postcolonial fiction, phonetics to applied linguistics, and Shakespeare to ELT methodology. Seminars are discussion-based and writing-intensive; assessment rewards argument, not memorisation. A language and phonetics laboratory supports pronunciation and listening to global norms.

Graduates thrive in education, media, corporate communication, the development sector and graduate study abroad. The M.A. deepens literary and linguistic scholarship for teachers and researchers, and the department's literary society and drama club keep the campus's cultural life loud and literate.

Purpose

Vision & Mission

Vision

A department where graduates read deeply, write clearly and speak with confidence on any stage in the world.

Mission

  1. 01 Teach literature and linguistics as complementary, mutually enriching disciplines.
  2. 02 Develop advanced communicative competence in speaking, writing and presentation.
  3. 03 Prepare English teachers and ELT professionals for schools, colleges and training centres.
  4. 04 Foster critical, cultural and ethical literacy through debate, drama and publication.
