The Academic Estate

A Curriculum Built for the Century Ahead

As the first private university in the Khulna Division, First Capital University of Bangladesh carries a simple conviction: a world-class education should not require leaving home. Our faculties unite rigorous curricula, full-time faculty and modern laboratories on one calm, politics-free campus in Chuadanga.

Learning here follows the international semester convention — two regular terms and a summer session — with continuous assessment, credit transfer provisions and degrees recognised by the University Grants Commission. Whether you arrive for engineering, business, law or the sciences, you graduate having practised your profession, not merely studied it.

The Framework

How a Degree Is Built

Semester System

Two regular semesters each year — Spring and Fall — plus a short summer term for retakes and grade improvements.

Credit Structure

Honours degrees of 132–160 credits completed across eight semesters; most courses carry 3 credits of lectures, labs and viva.

Grading Scale

A uniform UGC 4.00 scale — letter grades from A+ (80% and above) to F — with CGPA reviewed and published every semester.

Continuous Assessment

Mid-terms, assignments, lab reports, presentations and finals — no single examination decides your grade.
