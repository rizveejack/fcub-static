Eligibility

Do You Qualify?

Admission standards at FCUB follow the national framework — clear, published and applied without exception. Find your pathway below.

Undergraduate (Hons.)

SSC and HSC, or equivalent examinations, with a minimum GPA of 2.50 in each. A science background is required for CSE, EEE and Agriculture; any group is admitted to the other honours programs.

O-Level & A-Level

A minimum of five O-Level and two A-Level subjects with grade C or above. A University Grants Commission equivalence certificate must accompany the application.

Graduate (Master's)

A bachelor degree in a relevant field with at least a second class or CGPA 2.25 on the 4.00 scale. Professional experience strengthens MBA and MPH applications.

Diploma Engineers

BTEB diploma holders may enter the third semester of B.Sc. engineering programs under lateral-entry provisions, subject to seat availability and transcript review.

The Process

Five Steps to Your First Lecture

01

Collect the Form

Collect the admission form from the campus admission desk or the Chuadanga city office — or apply by post. Processing fee: ৳ 500.

02

Submit Documents

Submit the completed form with attested academic transcripts, certificates, photographs and a guardian's NID copy.

03

Admission Test & Interview

Sit the written admission test (English, general knowledge and basic mathematics) and attend the short viva with the department.

04

Selection & Offer

Merit lists are published within seven working days. Selected candidates receive an offer letter by SMS and email.

05

Enroll & Orient

Pay the admission and first-semester dues, complete registration and join the Spring orientation week on campus.

Forms are issued during office hours (Sunday–Thursday, 9:00–16:00) and the admission desk accepts submissions up to the published deadline each semester.

Scholarships & Waivers

Excellence Should Never Wait

We ensure every student receives the support to complete their degree — six standing waivers, awarded transparently each semester.

10–100%

Merit Scholarships

Tuition waivers scaled to SSC and HSC results — 100% for a golden GPA 5.00 in both, then 50%, 30% and 20% tiers. Renewed every semester with a CGPA of 3.50.

20%

Sibling Waiver

When two siblings study concurrently at FCUB, the second enjoys a standing 20% tuition waiver for the full duration of the program.

25%

Freedom Fighter Quota

Children and grandchildren of recognised freedom fighters receive a 25% tuition waiver, honouring the families of the nation's Liberation War.

Up to 30%

Sports & Cultural Excellence

National-level athletes, debaters and artists may earn a waiver of up to 30% on the recommendation of the university's cultural and sports committee.

Up to 40%

Need-Based Financial Aid

Assessed case-by-case with discretion and dignity — up to 40% tuition support so that a family's circumstances never decide a student's future.

Up to 25%

Women in STEM Bursary

Female students entering CSE, EEE, Public Health and Agriculture may claim a bursary of up to 25%, supporting women's leadership in technical fields.

Before You Submit

The Document Checklist

Bring every item below to the admission desk — attested photocopies are accepted at submission; originals are verified at enrollment.

  • Completed application form (campus copy or online printout)
  • SSC & HSC mark sheets and certificates — attested photocopies
  • O-Level & A-Level certificates with UGC equivalence, if applicable
  • Bachelor transcript and provisional certificate — graduate applicants
  • Six passport-size and two stamp-size colour photographs (recent)
  • National ID or birth certificate copy — applicant and guardian
  • Character certificate from the institution last attended
  • Supporting waiver documents — freedom fighter, sports or quota claims
