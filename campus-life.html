Student Life

A Campus Without Distraction

FCUB was founded on a simple conviction: a serious education needs a serene campus. Ours is proudly politics-free — no storms, no shutdowns, no wasted semesters. Just classrooms, clubs and a community that pulls in one direction.

That calm is anything but quiet. With more than eight societies, a packed cultural calendar and faculty available full-time, life here is a rehearsal for leadership — where our learners become leaders.

  • Politics-free since our founding in 2014
  • Weekly leave on Sunday & Monday
  • 8+ clubs and societies across four interests
  • Ghoramara, Alamdanga Road, Chuadanga-7200
