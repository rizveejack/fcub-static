About FCUB

Our Campus, Our Pride.

First Capital University of Bangladesh (FCUB) is the first private university in Khulna Division — built around excellent computer labs, a rich library, spacious and well-ventilated classrooms, and a congenial, politics-free academic environment where highly educated and experienced teachers are available full-time.

At present the university runs five faculties — Science & Engineering Technology, Law, Business Administration, Arts & Social Science, and Agriculture — shaping more than two thousand students into the leaders of tomorrow.

  • Excellent computer labs & rich library
  • Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms
  • Politics-free academic environment
  • Full-time, highly educated faculty
