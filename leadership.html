Board of Trustees

Custodians of the university’s endowment, estate and long-term vision — chaired by the Founder & Chairman.

02

Academic Council

The highest academic body: curricula, admission standards and examination policy across all eight departments.

03

Syndicate

The executive arm of governance, translating council policy into day-to-day academic practice.

Principles

How We Lead

Governance at FCUB is not a chart of committees — it is a set of promises, reviewed at every council meeting and honoured in every classroom.

  1. 01

    Academic Freedom

    Faculty and students pursue inquiry without fear or favour — debate is encouraged here, dogma is not.

  2. 02

    Transparency

    From admission scores to examination results, every process is documented, published and open to scrutiny.

  3. 03

    Student-First

    Every policy is tested against one question — what serves our learners best? Decisions begin and end with students.

  4. 04

    Meritocracy

    Scholarships, positions and honours are earned, never given. Merit is the only currency of advancement at FCUB.
